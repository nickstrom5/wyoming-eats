Steakhouses and cookouts

5 places for Wyoming beef and game: steakhouses in an old general store, on a fort's grounds and in a wildlife museum, and a fondue cookout under the Wind River Range. Each was checked in fall 2026.

Beef, bison and elk

The Gun Barrel in Jackson cooks elk loin and bison over an open flame among the old Wyoming Wildlife Museum's mounts; Buffalo Jump in Beulah dry-ages its steaks in an 1890s general store; Cavalryman in Laramie sits on the grounds of Fort Sanders, an 1866 post. Pitchfork Fondue near Pinedale runs Thursdays to Saturdays in summer.

The list at a glance

One each in Beulah, Cheyenne, Jackson, Laramie and Pinedale.

Our method: a place made the list only if a 2025 or 2026 source backed it up (a dated Travel Wyoming article, or Travel Wyoming's own listing for it) and its street address matched. No current source, or a website gone dark, and it stayed off. Review sites played no part, and places run by county and town rather than by anybody's score. Small towns keep short hours and the park lodges open only in season, so check before you drive.

On your phone, Cowboy State Eats orders this list by how far each place is from you, and a tap opens Apple Maps' live card with hours, photos and directions.

Albany County

1 place, by town.

  1. Cavalryman Steakhouse

    4425 S 3rd St, Laramie

    Steakhouse

    Prime rib, bison London broil and Wyoming soda bread on the grounds of Fort Sanders, an 1866 post.

Crook County

1 place, by town.

  1. Buffalo Jump Steakhouse

    5877 Old Hwy 14, Beulah

    Steakhouse

    Dry-aged steaks in an 1890s general store with a stone fireplace and creaking floors.

Laramie County

1 place, by town.

  1. Little Bear Inn

    1700 I-25, Cheyenne

    Steakhouse

    A steakhouse with a stone fireplace, a wagon wheel and mounted bears, moose and elk.

Sublette County

1 place, by town.

  1. Pitchfork Fondue Western Cookout

    9888 US-191, Pinedale

    Steakhouse

    Meat and vegetables cooked outdoors over a wood fire, with views of the Wind River Range, Thursdays to Saturdays in summer.

Teton County

1 place, by town.

  1. Gun Barrel Steak and Game House

    862 W Broadway, Jackson

    Steakhouse
