Farm to table

7 kitchens that cook from Wyoming's ranches, farms and game, one of them a James Beard semifinalist. Each was checked in fall 2026.

From the ranch

The Middle Fork in Lander buys from Lander's own farms; Gather in Jackson grows its own microgreens; Turpin Meadow Ranch near Moran cooks venison, elk and bison in an 1887 ranch's dining room. Le Rêve in Sheridan was a James Beard Best New Restaurant semifinalist in 2025.

The list at a glance

Towns with the most: Jackson (2), Sheridan (1), Riverton (1), Laramie (1), Lander (1), Moran (1).

Our method: a place made the list only if a 2025 or 2026 source backed it up (a dated Travel Wyoming article, or Travel Wyoming's own listing for it) and its street address matched. No current source, or a website gone dark, and it stayed off. Review sites played no part, and places run by county and town rather than by anybody's score. Small towns keep short hours and the park lodges open only in season, so check before you drive.

On your phone, Cowboy State Eats orders this list by how far each place is from you, and a tap opens Apple Maps' live card with hours, photos and directions.
