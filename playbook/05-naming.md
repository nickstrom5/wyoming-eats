# Cowboy State Eats: name, icon and palette (provisional, 2026-11-05; Nick decides)

## Name
- **Cowboy State Eats** (App Store name "Cowboy State Eats: Wyoming Restaurants", Home Screen "Cowboy State Eats"). "The Cowboy State"
  is Wyoming's best-known nickname. Web searches (2026-11-05) found no app, show, magazine or blog called "Cowboy State Eats". Near
  names to weigh: Cowboy State Daily (a Wyoming news site, not food) and the University of Wyoming Cowboys (athletics).
- **Wyoming Eats** is free too (the searches found only scraped "Wyoming Eats: … McDonald's" pages, no app or account), but the
  University of Wyoming Extension runs **"Eat Wyoming"**, a local-food directory, which is close. **307 Eats** (the area code) is an
  alternative with no clash found.
- Home Screen: at 17 characters, "Cowboy State Eats" will likely show as "Cowboy State…" under the icon, as "West Virginia Eats" and
  "Old Dominion Eats" do. A shorter Home Screen name ("Cowboy Eats", "WY Eats") is Nick's call.
- Avoided: the bucking horse and rider (the State of Wyoming's registered trademark, licensed by the state), "Forever West" (the
  Wyoming Office of Tourism's slogan), the University of Wyoming's names and marks.

## Palette (a landscape one: the University of Wyoming's brown and gold read as the Cowboys')
sagebrush #3E4F3A (8.8:1 on white) for titles and tint; red-rock rust #8C3B2A (white on it 7.6:1) for the hand-checked guide cards;
Teton blue #2B5C8A (white on it 7.0:1) for honors chips; prairie wheat #D9A441 only as a fill under coal #1E1E1E (7.4:1); cream
#F6F1E7. Header band: the Tetons' jagged line in Teton blue, red-rock foothills, the sagebrush plains, over a prairie-wheat baseline.
